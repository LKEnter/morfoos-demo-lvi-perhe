# Product Requirements

## Goal

Demo marketing website for **Perheputki Etelä Oy**, a fictional family-owned plumbing, HVAC and excavation company operating in Southern Finland. Used for Morfoos LVI outreach demos — not a real business.

## Audience

Homeowners in the Helsinki metropolitan area and Southern Finland who need plumbing, heating or drainage work. Housing companies and property managers coordinating larger renovation or piping projects. Businesses needing dependable commercial-scale LVI or excavation work.

## Success

A visitor who, within seconds, believes this is a real, established, still-active local company worth calling — and then does.

## Product Notes

- Primary CTA: "Pyydä tarjous" (Request a quote). Secondary CTA: "Soita meille" (Call us).
- The line a visitor remembers after 10 seconds: "This family has been taking care of homes in our area for years — and they still show up."
- Proof on hand: illustrative placeholder reviews (not real Google Business Profile data).
- Local specificity uses generic service areas (pääkaupunkiseutu / Etelä-Suomi) rather than identifiable real towns.
- Every screen keeps the call-to-action (quote or phone) within easy reach, especially on mobile.

## Accessibility

WCAG 2.1 AA as the baseline: ≥4.5:1 body text contrast, ≥3:1 for large text/UI components, full keyboard operability, visible focus states, semantic landmarks and heading order, alt text on all imagery. Respect `prefers-reduced-motion` throughout.
